Size Guide

How to Measure

For accurate measurements, follow these steps:

  • Bust: Measure around the fullest part of your bust, keeping the tape parallel to the floor
  • Waist: Measure around your natural waistline, keeping the tape comfortably loose
  • Hip: Measure around the fullest part of your hips, approximately 8" below your waist
  • Shoulder: Measure from the edge of one shoulder to the edge of the other shoulder across the back
  • Length: Measure from the shoulder (where it meets the neck) straight down to desired length

Tip: Always measure over the undergarments you plan to wear with the outfit.

Fit Guide & Recommendations

  • Between sizes? We recommend sizing up for a more comfortable fit
  • Fitted styles: Choose your exact size or size down if you prefer a snug fit
  • Flowy/Loose styles: Choose your regular size for the intended drape
  • Fabric consideration: Silk and satin have less stretch - size up if in doubt
  • Cotton and georgette: More forgiving fabrics with natural give

Important Notes:

• All measurements are in inches and may vary by ±1 inch due to the handcrafted nature of our garments

• Custom sizing and alterations are available - please contact us for details

• Sizes may vary slightly between different styles and fabric types

• For best results, measure yourself before ordering or consult with us
