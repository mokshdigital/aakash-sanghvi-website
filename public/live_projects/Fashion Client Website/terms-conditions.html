Size Guide

How to Measure

For accurate measurements, follow these steps:

  • Bust: Measure around the fullest part of your bust, keeping the tape parallel to the floor
  • Waist: Measure around your natural waistline, keeping the tape comfortably loose
  • Hip: Measure around the fullest part of your hips, approximately 8" below your waist
  • Shoulder: Measure from the edge of one shoulder to the edge of the other shoulder across the back
  • Length: Measure from the shoulder (where it meets the neck) straight down to desired length

Tip: Always measure over the undergarments you plan to wear with the outfit.

Fit Guide & Recommendations

  • Between sizes? We recommend sizing up for a more comfortable fit
  • Fitted styles: Choose your exact size or size down if you prefer a snug fit
  • Flowy/Loose styles: Choose your regular size for the intended drape
  • Fabric consideration: Silk and satin have less stretch - size up if in doubt
  • Cotton and georgette: More forgiving fabrics with natural give

Important Notes:

• All measurements are in inches and may vary by ±1 inch due to the handcrafted nature of our garments

• Custom sizing and alterations are available - please contact us for details

• Sizes may vary slightly between different styles and fabric types

• For best results, measure yourself before ordering or consult with us

Saree Specifications

Standard Saree Measurements:

  • Saree Length: 5.5 to 6 meters (standard for most draping styles)
  • Saree Width: 42 to 47 inches
  • Blouse Piece: Unstitched blouse fabric of 0.8 to 1 meter included
  • Border Width: Varies by design (typically 2-6 inches)
  • Pallu Length: Approximately 1 to 1.25 meters

Note: Sarees are one-size-fits-all. The blouse requires stitching as per your measurements.

Blouse Size Chart

The saree comes with unstitched blouse fabric. Use these measurements for stitching:

Size Label Bust (inches) Waist (inches) Shoulder (inches) Blouse Length (inches)
32 XS 32-33 24-25 14 14-15
34 S 34-35 26-27 14.5 15-16
36 M 36-37 28-29 15 15-16
38 L 38-39 30-31 15.5 16-17
40 XL 40-41 32-33 16 16-17
42 XXL 42-44 34-36 16.5 17-18
44 3XL 44-46 36-38 17 17-18

Draping & Fit Tips

  • Height consideration: Standard 5.5-6m sarees work for heights 5'2" to 5'8"
  • Petite (under 5'2"): May need to adjust pleats or pallu length
  • Tall (over 5'8"): Consider 6.5m sarees for full draping
  • Fabric type: Lightweight fabrics are easier to drape and manage
  • Blouse fit: We recommend professional tailoring for the perfect fit

Important Notes:

• Saree measurements may vary by ±2 inches due to handloom/handcrafted nature

• Blouse piece is unstitched - tailoring required (fabric provided)

• We can recommend trusted tailors in your area - just ask!

• Pre-stitched blouse option available on request (additional charges apply)

How to Measure for Lehenga

For accurate lehenga measurements:

  • Bust: Measure around the fullest part of your bust for the choli/blouse
  • Waist: Measure around your natural waistline where the lehenga will sit
  • Hip: Measure around the fullest part of your hips
  • Choli Length: From shoulder to desired length (usually to waist or slightly below)
  • Lehenga Length: From waist to floor (usually 40-44 inches)
  • Dupatta: Standard length is 2.25 to 2.5 meters

Tip: Measure where you intend to wear the lehenga (natural waist or slightly higher).

Lehenga Fit Guide

  • Choli fit: Should be fitted but comfortable - size up if between sizes
  • Lehenga waist: Should sit comfortably at your waist with elastic/drawstring
  • Lehenga flare: Our lehengas have standard 3-4 meter flare for elegant draping
  • Height adjustment: Lehenga length can be adjusted by altering the waist position
  • Petticoat: A matching petticoat is recommended for proper shape and volume

Important Notes:

• All measurements may vary by ±1 inch due to handcrafted nature

• Custom sizing available for all lehenga components (choli, skirt, dupatta)

• Semi-stitched lehengas can be adjusted for perfect fit - alterations recommended

• Dupatta length is standard but can be customized on request

How to Measure for Western Wear

Standard western wear measurements:

  • Bust: Measure around the fullest part of your bust, keeping tape parallel to floor
  • Waist: Measure around your natural waistline
  • Hip: Measure around the fullest part of your hips (approximately 8" below waist)
  • Inseam (for pants): Measure from crotch to ankle along the inner leg
  • Length (for tops): Measure from shoulder point to desired hem length

Tip: Wear form-fitting undergarments while measuring for accuracy.

Western Wear Fit Guide

  • Tops/Blouses: Should skim your body without being too tight
  • Dresses: Consider length and whether you'll wear heels
  • Pants: Should sit comfortably at natural waist or hips depending on style
  • Between sizes: Size up for comfort, especially with fitted styles
  • Stretchy fabrics: Can accommodate ±1 size variation
  • Structured fabrics: Less forgiving - choose exact size

Important Notes:

• Western wear sizing follows standard international measurements

• Measurements may vary by ±1 inch depending on fabric and style
