# UCW Peer Tutoring - Project Documentation

## 📌 Project Overview
**Client:** University Canada West (Learning Commons)
**Role:** Lead UX & Product Designer
**Context:** Redesigning the peer tutoring booking experience to reduce drop-offs and improve accessibility.

## 🎯 The Challenge
- **High Friction:** Students took ~5 mins to book a session.
- **Cognitive Overload:** Original design was a "Scroll of Death" with too much text and unstructured steps.
- **Anxiety:** No clear confirmation, leading to students emailing to check if it worked.

## 💡 The Solution: "4-Step Guided Flow"
We replaced the long scroll with a wizard-style interface.
1.  **Choose Subject:** Icon-driven selection.
2.  **Select Tutor:** Profile cards with ratings and bio.
3.  **Pick Time:** Calendar integration (Calendly/Outlook style).
4.  **Confirm:** Success page with "Add to Calendar" button.

## 📊 Impact & Outcomes
- **60% Faster Booking:** Time reduced from 5 min -> ~2 min.
- **40% Drop-off Reduction:** Completion rates soared.
- **100% Confirmation Clarity:** "Did it work?" emails dropped to near zero.

## 🧠 UX Methods Used
- **User Personas:** "International Student needing English Help".
- **Journey Mapping:** Identifying the anxiety points in the old flow.
- **Usability Testing:** Verified the new 4-step wizard with actual students.
- **Information Architecture:** Grouping tutors by subject rather than a random list.
