The Narrative

A Story of Survival & Scale

01 March 2020

The Growth Ceiling

March 2020 — The Day the Classrooms Closed. For ESPI, a business built on physical classrooms and face-to-face coaching, the lockdown was an existential threat. Our centers were empty, but the ambition of thousands of students to study abroad hadn't vanished — it was just trapped.

As Lead Business Analyst and Product Lead, I faced a stark reality: adapt or die.

"The world stopped. But the ambition of thousands of students hadn't vanished — it was just trapped."
02 The 60-Day Sprint

The BA & Product Strategy

We didn't have a year for discovery. I led a "War Room" development cycle, translating the chaotic needs of remote education into a structured SaaS architecture.

May 2020: We launched the MVP. It was a lifeline that kept the business alive.

October 2020: Following a rapid feedback loop from our first 1,000 users, we released V1.0 — featuring the modular curriculum and automated assessment engine.

[IMAGE_ADMIN_DASHBOARD]
03 December 2020

The B2B SaaS Evolution

By year-end, we realized we had built something bigger than ourselves. I led the Whitelabeling initiative, transitioning the codebase into a true B2B SaaS model.

Other institutions could now skin our platform and logic for their own students. This pivot allowed us to scale to 20+ agents across the Asian subcontinent almost overnight.

What started as a survival tactic in March ended the year as a market-leading platform with 5,000+ paid students.
