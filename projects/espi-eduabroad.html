Core Workflows

01. STUDENT EXPERIENCE

Seamless Onboarding & Engagement

We replaced paper forms with digital OTP verification. Students could instantly view their matched universities, book IELTS demos, and take scholarship tests.

  • → WhatsApp OTP Verification
  • → Automated Scholarship Scoring
  • → Instant Slot Booking for Reps
02. TRIAGE SYSTEM

Smart Matchmaking

Counselors acted as the intake layer, enriching student profiles and "triaging" them to the correct university representatives based on eligibility, ensuring high-quality leads for universities.

03. REP CRM

Pocket CRM for Universities

University reps ditched notepads for a dedicated tablet interface. They could see their specific leads, view academic history, and log interaction remarks instantly.

Under the Hood

Adalo No-Code & Database

Leveraged Adalo's relational database to handle complex many-to-many relationships (Students <-> Universities <-> Counselors). This allowed for rapid iteration without writing boilerplate backend code.

Automated Logic

Scholarship Engine: Custom logic automatically calculated waivers based on test scores (1-30 scale), instantly reflecting the award on the student's dashboard.
